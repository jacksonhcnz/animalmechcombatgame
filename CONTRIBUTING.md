# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Please read these rules first.

## Licences

- Code you contribute is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).
- Art and audio you contribute under `assets/` are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](assets/LICENSE).

## No AI-generated art

Do not submit AI-generated images, textures, models, concept art, audio, icons or fonts. By submitting art or audio you confirm it is your own original work, or open-licensed work you have the right to use.

## Art style

- Mechs: plain flat colours, no textures.
- Levels: low-poly, blocky shapes in flat colours.

## Third-party assets

Only use assets under open licences compatible with this project. Read and understand the licence first. Add an entry to [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) in the same change. If the licence is unclear, ask before using the asset.

## Sign-off (Developer Certificate of Origin)

Sign off every commit to certify the [DCO](DCO.md):

```
git commit -s -m "Describe your change"
```

This adds a `Signed-off-by:` line with your name and email. There is no contributor licence agreement, and contributions stay under the licences above.

## Balance changes

Gameplay numbers live in data files. Balance changes are proposed as edits to those files and ship with game updates.
